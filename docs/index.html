Nothing to Declare?

Every medical paper arrives with a declaration, often just "The authors declare no conflicts of interest." Journals rarely check the bag. We scanned each declaration against the US government's public record of what drug and device companies paid each doctor.

    Secondary inspection queue

      Arrival

      Open-access papers in 40 clinical journals with US authors. Each declaration is read from the full text.

      Passport check

      Each US author is matched to one doctor in the public payments record by name, state and city. Ambiguous matches are left out.

      X-ray

      Payments companies reported for that doctor in the 3 calendar years before publication, grouped by company and type.

      Channel

      Red when a paying company, or its corporate group, is missing from the declaration. Partial declarations are read by an AI model.

      A red channel is a question for an editor, not a finding of misconduct. Journal policies differ on which relationships must be declared; some payments may have been disclosed to the editor but not printed. Only US clinicians are covered by the payment record, so non-US authors are not checked. Authors are anonymised: specialty and US region only, amounts rounded to the nearest $1,000. Payment data: CMS Open Payments, program years 2020–2025. A hand check of 20 random red cases found about 9 in 10 correct.
